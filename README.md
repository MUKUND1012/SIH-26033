# Smart India Hackathon Workshop
# Date:30.10.26
## Reference Number:26019264
## Name:MUKUND KM

## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

1.1 Detailed explanation of the proposed solution

- Develop an AI-powered digital marketplace connecting farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Allow farmers to register and upload their agricultural products, quantity, price and harvest date.
- Enable consumers, retailers, hotels and supermarkets to purchase directly from farmers.
- Allow FPOs (Farmer Producer Organizations) to combine products from multiple farmers for bulk selling.
- Integrate logistics services to connect farmers with nearby transport providers.
- Use AI to predict future crop demand based on seasonal trends, previous sales and weather conditions.
- Implement smart route optimization to reduce transportation costs and delivery time.
- Provide transparent pricing so farmers can compare different buyer offers.
- Introduce QR-based product tracking to monitor agricultural products from farm to customer.
- Support regional languages and voice-based product listing for easy farmer access.
- 
1.2 How it addresses the problem
  
- Reduces the number of unnecessary intermediaries.
- Connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Helps farmers receive better prices through competitive offers.
- Reduces consumer prices by minimizing additional supply-chain costs.
- Improves transportation through route optimization.
- Prevents overproduction using AI-based demand forecasting.
- Reduces food wastage through better supply and demand coordination.
- Provides transparent market information to farmers.
